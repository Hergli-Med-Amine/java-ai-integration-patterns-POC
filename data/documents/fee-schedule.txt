Fee Schedule for Private Clients
Fictional document written for a proof of concept. Not financial advice.

1. Management fee
The annual management fee is charged quarterly on the average portfolio value of the quarter.
Portfolio value up to EUR 250,000: 1.00% per year.
Portfolio value from EUR 250,000 to EUR 1,000,000: 0.80% per year.
Portfolio value above EUR 1,000,000: 0.60% per year.
The rate of the band reached applies to the whole portfolio value.

2. Custody fee
Securities custody costs 0.15% per year of the value of securities held, with a minimum of EUR 100 per year. Cash balances are not subject to the custody fee.

3. Transaction fees
Equities and exchange-traded products: 0.25% of the order value, minimum EUR 25 per order.
Bonds: 0.15% of the order value, minimum EUR 25 per order.
Investment funds: no transaction fee for funds managed by the bank, 0.50% for third-party funds.
Crypto exchange-traded products: 0.50% of the order value, minimum EUR 25 per order.

4. Other fees
Account opening and closing are free of charge.
Transfer of securities to another bank: EUR 50 per position.
Paper statements: EUR 5 per statement. Electronic statements are free of charge.
