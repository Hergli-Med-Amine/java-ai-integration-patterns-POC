Investment Policy for Discretionary and Advisory Mandates
Fictional document written for a proof of concept. Not financial advice.

1. Purpose
This policy sets the limits within which client portfolios are built and monitored. It applies to every client mandate, whatever the portfolio size. Where a portfolio is outside these limits, the relationship manager contacts the client and agrees a plan to bring it back within limits.

2. Risk profiles
Each client is assigned one risk profile after a suitability assessment. The profile is reviewed every two years, or earlier if the client's situation changes.
CONSERVATIVE: capital preservation first. Equities and funds between 0% and 30% of portfolio value, bonds between 50% and 90%, cash between 5% and 30%.
BALANCED: long-term growth with moderate fluctuations. Equities and funds between 30% and 60%, bonds between 20% and 60%, cash between 2% and 20%.
GROWTH: long-term growth, large fluctuations accepted. Equities and funds between 60% and 95%, bonds up to 30%, cash between 1% and 15%.

3. Concentration limits
A single issuer may not exceed 10% of portfolio value, except government and supranational bonds rated AA or better.
A single sector may not exceed 35% of portfolio value for CONSERVATIVE and BALANCED clients, and 50% for GROWTH clients. Broad index funds count as DIVERSIFIED and are not attributed to individual sectors.
A single region outside the client's home region may not exceed 60% of portfolio value.

4. Crypto-assets
Crypto-assets are permitted only for clients with the GROWTH risk profile.
Exposure to crypto-assets may not exceed 5% of portfolio value.
Only exchange-traded products backed by the underlying asset are permitted. The bank does not hold crypto-assets directly on behalf of clients and does not offer custody of private keys.
Clients must acknowledge the crypto-asset risk disclosure before the first purchase.

5. Monitoring and rebalancing
Portfolios are checked against these limits every business day. A breach caused by market movements is reviewed at the next quarterly meeting. A breach caused by a client instruction must be documented and acknowledged by the client in writing.
